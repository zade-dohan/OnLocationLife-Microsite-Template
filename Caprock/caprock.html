Life on Site

Community Code of Conduct

At Caprock, we're committed to fostering a community where every guest feels respected, safe, and at home. Our Community Guidelines reflect the shared values that make that possible: care for one another, care for our shared spaces, and a spirit of cooperation throughout your stay.

  • Occupants must behave in an orderly, respectful manner at all times.
  • Respect others' rights, safety, and privacy and avoid inappropriate conduct.
  • Keep all areas clean and well-maintained.
  • Treat facilities and property with care.
  • Follow directions and cooperate with management, security, and authorized staff.
  • Report any issues or concerns promptly to management.
  • Harassment or discrimination is strictly prohibited based on protected characteristics (race, gender, religion, disability, etc.).
  • All reports are investigated thoroughly.
  • Violators may face discipline up to removal from housing and employer notification.
  • Individuals who report concerns or participate in investigations in good faith are protected from retaliation.
